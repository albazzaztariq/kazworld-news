What is real and what is generated

There is no presenter: the voice is ElevenLabs text-to-speech reading plain English summaries I wrote from the linked articles, and the visuals are stock photos with key facts. Kazakh headlines under each English headline are the originals. Stories: Tengrinews, Egemen Qazaqstan, Sputnik Qazaqstan (21 October 2026). Ticker: Tengrinews English. Weather: Open-Meteo. Map outlines: Natural Earth. Cutaway photos: Pexels. The road report covers Almaty and Astana only and uses recent road news because live traffic data is not connected. Please check summaries against the original articles before any public use.
